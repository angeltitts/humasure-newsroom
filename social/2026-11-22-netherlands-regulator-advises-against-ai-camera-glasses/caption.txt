Dutch privacy regulator advises against buying AI camera glasses after tests

The Netherlands' data protection authority tested six pairs of AI camera glasses and found unreliable safety advice and easily hidden recording lights.

Why it matters: D5 Private by default, D3 Physical safety, D4 Honest identity.
