Privacy regulators worldwide back safeguards for AI agents, including a way to stop them

The Global Privacy Assembly, led by Canada's privacy commissioner, adopted a resolution asking for pause and undo functions, logging and human review for AI agents.

Why it matters: D1 Always stoppable, D2 Bounded autonomy, D5 Private by default.
