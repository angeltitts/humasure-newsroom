Researchers show how a desktop AI robot could be taken over on a home network

Security researchers found flaws that could let someone on the same network run code on the Reachy Mini robot and use its camera, microphone and motors. Updates are available.

Why it matters: D6 Secure and supported, D5 Private by default, D1 Always stoppable.

Full story and sources at humasure.org/news (link in bio).
Sources: JFrog Security Research; INCIBE-CERT (Spain); Pollen Robotics on GitHub.
