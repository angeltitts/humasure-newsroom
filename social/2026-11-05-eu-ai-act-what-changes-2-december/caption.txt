EU AI Act: what changes on 2 December 2026, and what does not

On 2 December 2026 the EU AI Act bans AI that makes non-consensual intimate images or child abuse material, and older generative AI must start marking its output as AI-made.

Why it matters: D4 Honest identity, D7 No manipulation, D9 Accountable.
