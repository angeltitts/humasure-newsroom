EU asks for comments on a template for checking how high-risk AI affects people's rights

The European Commission has published a draft form for the fundamental rights impact assessment required by the EU AI Act. Comments are open until 15 January 2027.

Why it matters: D8 Fair treatment, D9 Accountable, D1 Always stoppable.
