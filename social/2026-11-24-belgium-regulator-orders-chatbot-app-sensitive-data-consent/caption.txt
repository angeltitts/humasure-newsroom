Belgian regulator: chatbot app needs explicit consent to learn from sensitive chats

Belgium's data protection authority has reprimanded an unnamed chatbot app and given it four months to stop using health, religion and similar details from conversations to improve its AI without explicit consent.

Why it matters: D5 Private by default, D9 Accountable.
