South Korea sets rules for writing AI laws: fix existing laws first, act fast on harm

South Korea's national AI committee agreed on 6 November that new AI rules should build on the AI Basic Act, with fast action when AI threatens life or safety.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D3 Physical safety, D6 Secure and supported.
