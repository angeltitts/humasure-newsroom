Singapore sets AI risk rules for banks and insurers, with human oversight built in

Singapore's financial regulator has issued AI risk management guidelines for all financial institutions. They take effect on 7 October 2027, with some parts phased in to 2028.

Why it matters: D1 Always stoppable, D2 Bounded autonomy, D9 Accountable.
