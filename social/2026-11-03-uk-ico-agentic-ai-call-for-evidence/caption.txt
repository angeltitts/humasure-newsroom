UK data regulator asks how AI agents should follow data protection law

The UK Information Commissioner's Office wants evidence on AI agents that act with some autonomy. Responses close on 20 November 2026 and will shape a code of practice.

Why it matters: D2 Bounded autonomy, D5 Private by default, D9 Accountable.
