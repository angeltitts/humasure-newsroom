India's cyber agency sets four risk tiers for auditing AI systems, including agents

India's CERT-In published AI security auditing guidelines on 4 November 2026. Higher-risk systems, including some AI agents, face independent audits and must stay stoppable and reversible.

Why it matters: D1 Always stoppable, D2 Bounded autonomy, D6 Secure and supported.
