Brazil's child online safety guide treats AI chatbots as likely used by children

Guidance from Brazil's data protection authority on the country's Digital Statute of Children and Adolescents presumes that generative AI chatbots and games are likely to be used by under-18s.

Why it matters: D5 Private by default, D7 No manipulation, D9 Accountable.
