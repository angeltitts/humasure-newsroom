China publishes five humanoid robot standards, including intelligence levels

China's industry ministry has published five humanoid robot standards covering intelligence grading, joints, hands, training sites and training data. They apply from publication.

Why it matters: D3 Physical safety, D2 Bounded autonomy, D5 Private by default.
