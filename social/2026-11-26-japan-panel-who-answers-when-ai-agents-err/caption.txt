Japan sets up expert panel to decide who answers when an AI agent gets it wrong

A Japanese government panel met for the first time on 24 November 2026 to study legal liability for AI agents that shop, book or sign up on people's behalf. It aims to report by around June 2027.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D2 Bounded autonomy, D1 Always stoppable.
