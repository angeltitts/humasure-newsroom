UK privacy regulator sets out how data law applies to ads in AI chatbots

The ICO's AdTech 3.0 report says chatbots and AI agents bring no new data rules for advertising, but warns about sensitive guesses drawn from conversations.

Why it matters: D5 Private by default, D4 Honest identity, D7 No manipulation.
