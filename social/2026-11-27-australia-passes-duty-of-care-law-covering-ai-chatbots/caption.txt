Australia passes online duty of care law that covers AI chatbots

Australia's Parliament passed the Digital Duty of Care bill on 25 November 2026. Platforms and AI chatbots must assess and reduce foreseeable harms, with fines of up to 5% of global turnover.

Why it matters: D7 No manipulation, D9 Accountable, D5 Private by default.
