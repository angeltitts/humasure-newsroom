Dominican Republic sets binding AI ethics rules for its public services

A decree signed on 2 November 2026 bans some government uses of AI, requires impact assessments before high-impact systems go live, and gives people the right to ask for human review.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D8 Fair treatment, D4 Honest identity.
