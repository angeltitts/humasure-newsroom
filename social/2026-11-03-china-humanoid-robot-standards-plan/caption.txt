China plans at least 100 humanoid robot standards by 2028, including safety and ethics

A draft guideline from China's industry ministry sets a 2028 target for a national system of humanoid robot standards, with safety governance and ethics alongside performance.

Why it matters: D3 Physical safety, D9 Accountable, D10 Safe for its whole life.
