Study: AI agents acting for different people did worse than one shared agent

In simulated tests of five AI models, per-person agents sharing a budget, calendar or order stalled, overrode each other and made claims about people that had no support.

Why it matters: D2 Bounded autonomy, D4 Honest identity, D9 Accountable.
