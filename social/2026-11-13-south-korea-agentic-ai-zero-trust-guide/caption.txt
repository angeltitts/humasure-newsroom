South Korea issues zero-trust security guide for businesses using AI agents

South Korea's science ministry and internet security agency published a guide on 6 November 2026: check every action an AI agent takes, and have people approve the risky ones.

Why it matters: D2 Bounded autonomy, D6 Secure and supported, D1 Always stoppable.
