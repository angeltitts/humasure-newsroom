Australia: from 10 December, privacy policies must say when computers decide

From 10 December 2026, Australian organisations must explain in their privacy policies when computer programs, including AI, help make decisions that significantly affect people.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D5 Private by default, D8 Fair treatment.
