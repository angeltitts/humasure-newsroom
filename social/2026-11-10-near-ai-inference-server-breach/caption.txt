NEAR AI reports breach via flaw in AI serving software; 18 legacy agents' data accessed

NEAR AI says an attacker used a flaw in the open-source SGLang inference engine to reach a server, exposing some user details and data from 18 older AI agents. It has patched and rotated keys.

Why it matters: D5 Private by default, D6 Secure and supported, D9 Accountable.
