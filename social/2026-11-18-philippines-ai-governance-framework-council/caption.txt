Philippines adopts national AI governance framework and creates AI council

An executive order signed on 12 November 2026 sets six principles for AI, including safety and accountability, and creates a National AI Council. It adds no new penalties.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D3 Physical safety, D6 Secure and supported.
