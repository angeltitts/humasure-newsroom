Zimbabwe approves a national AI charter built on transparency and accountability

Zimbabwe's Cabinet approved a National Artificial Intelligence Charter on 17 November 2026. It sets ethical principles and a complaints route, but its full text is not yet public.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D8 Fair treatment, D5 Private by default.
