Netherlands sets out national AI vision and explores its own AI safety institute

The Dutch government approved a national AI vision on 6 November 2026. It promises to protect rights and public values and to study setting up a Dutch AI safety institute.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D1 Always stoppable, D8 Fair treatment.
