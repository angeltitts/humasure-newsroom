Singapore agencies tell the public how to use personal AI agents safely

Three Singapore government agencies issued a joint advisory on 6 November 2026: link only the accounts an agent needs, keep it away from sensitive accounts, and approve big actions yourself.

Why it matters: D2 Bounded autonomy, D1 Always stoppable, D5 Private by default.
