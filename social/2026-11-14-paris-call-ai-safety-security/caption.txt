Paris Call: 16 countries and 10 AI developers back shutdown plans and incident alerts

A voluntary declaration signed in Paris on 9 November commits governments and AI developers to shared testing, shutdown mechanisms and faster warnings about serious AI incidents.

Why it matters: D1 Always stoppable, D9 Accountable, D6 Secure and supported.
