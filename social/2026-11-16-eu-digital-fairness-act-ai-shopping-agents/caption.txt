EU proposal would let shoppers set limits on AI agents that buy for them

The European Commission's draft Digital Fairness Act, published 11 November 2026, would give people control over AI agents that make purchases and let them switch off addictive features.

Why it matters: D2 Bounded autonomy, D7 No manipulation, D1 Always stoppable.
