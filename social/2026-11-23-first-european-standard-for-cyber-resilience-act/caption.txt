EU publishes first European standard to support the Cyber Resilience Act

The first standard for the EU's connected-product security law sets shared terms and lifecycle security steps, from design to end of support. More than 30 others are on the way.

Why it matters: D6 Secure and supported, D10 Safe for its whole life, D9 Accountable.
