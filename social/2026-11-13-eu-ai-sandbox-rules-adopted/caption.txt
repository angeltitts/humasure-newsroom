EU adopts common rules for AI sandboxes: free for small firms, risky tests can be stopped

New EU rules published on 11 November 2026 set how supervised AI test spaces work: who can join, what a test plan must cover, and when a regulator can halt a project.

Why it matters: D1 Always stoppable, D3 Physical safety, D9 Accountable.
