Australian researchers find Bluetooth trailer brakes could be applied by anyone nearby

Researchers in Tasmania found that some Bluetooth trailer brake controllers accepted brake commands without pairing. The maker has released fixes for two models; one older model is still waiting.

Why it matters: D6 Secure and supported, D3 Physical safety, D1 Always stoppable.
