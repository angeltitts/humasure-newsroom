EU AI Office picks outside team to test advanced AI models for cyber-attack risks

A consortium led by Capgemini will help the European Commission's AI Office model and assess the cyber-offence risks of the most capable general-purpose AI models under the EU AI Act.

Why it matters: D6 Secure and supported, D9 Accountable, D10 Safe for its whole life.
