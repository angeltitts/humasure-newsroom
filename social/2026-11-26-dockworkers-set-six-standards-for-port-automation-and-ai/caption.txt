Dockworkers' council sets six minimum standards for port automation and AI

At its assembly in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, an international dockworkers' body adopted six minimum standards for bargaining over port automation and AI, including health and safety with real accountability.

Why it matters: D9 Accountable, D3 Physical safety, D2 Bounded autonomy.
