US worker safety institute asks how to keep people safe around humanoid robots

NIOSH, the US federal worker safety research institute, is asking the public which research on humanoid robots at work should come first. Comments are open until late February 2027.

Why it matters: D3 Physical safety, D1 Always stoppable, D2 Bounded autonomy.
